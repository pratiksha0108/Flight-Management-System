THE PRODUCT STORY

One journey. Two perspectives.

Travelers need a clear booking flow. Operations teams need trustworthy inventory. Boarding Lab connects both sides so you can see the effect of each decision.

Try the whole loop

  1. Search Chicago to New York with two travelers.
  2. Choose two seats and create a practice reservation.
  3. Open Control tower for the same date. See the seats reflected in occupancy.
  4. Try reducing capacity below an assigned seat. The system protects that booking.
  5. Cancel the reservation and watch those seats become available again.

What is real, and what is not?

The seat-selection and inventory logic works. The flights, dates, fares, small cabin layouts, and reservations are fictional. City codes are display labels, not exact airport identifiers. There are no airline connections, payments, passenger details, tickets or cross-user availability.

Cabin choice changes only the illustrative fare, not the seat layout. No taxes, fees, connections, time-zone conversion, or actual fare rules are modeled. Session data resets on reload.

Before this could be operational

A backend must provide transactional seat holds, expiry, idempotent booking, authentication, reconciliation with an airline, and careful handling of personal data. This prototype demonstrates interaction and state rules, not a production reservation system.
